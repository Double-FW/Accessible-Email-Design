# Related public guidance

These repositories were confirmed public through GitHub on 20 November 2026. This records visibility at inspection, not permanent availability, legal currency, content validation or rights to copy linked works. Select sources for the actual question and retain the exact revision and heading used.

| Need | Public resource | Contribution and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared meanings and evidence labels | [Terminology and Principles](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles) | Scoped decisions, glossary status and source-authority boundaries. Most individual wording remains proposed. |
| Typography, spacing and presentation | [Accessible Typography for UX and Brand Systems](https://github.com/Double-FW/Accessible-Typography-for-UX-and-Brand-Systems) | Contextual readability and brand considerations; not a guarantee from one font or source CSS value. |
| Clarity, processing demand and continuity | [Cognitive UX Design](https://github.com/Double-FW/Cognitive-UX-Design) | Meaning, orientation, task support and preference choices; do not infer diagnoses or remove necessary complexity automatically. |
| Experience and brand promise | [POUR for Branding Systems and CX](https://github.com/Double-FW/POUR-for-Branding-Systems-and-CX) | Satisfactory outcomes and emotional research lenses; participant findings remain distinct from technical conformance. |
| Structured testing and evidence planning | [QA Accessibility Testing](https://github.com/Double-FW/QA_Accessibility_Testing) | Scoped test methods and evidence. A web/browser check is not an actual email-client test or proof that every user completes a task. |
| Participant/recipient information | [Data Ethics and Disability](https://github.com/Double-FW/Data_Ethics_and_Disability) | Proportionate involvement, privacy and consent questions; the link provides no approved records system or legal basis. |
| Linked or attached publications | [Accessible Digital and Printed Documents](https://github.com/Double-FW/Accessible_Digital_and_Printed_Documents) | Structure, formats and delivered document usability. An attachment is not accessible merely because its source was reviewed. |
| Linked video and production coordination | [Accessible Film, TV and Video Production Management](https://github.com/Double-FW/Accessible-Film-TV-and-Video-Content-Production-Management) | Exact-version handoffs, access-service planning and published-path evidence. |
| Audio description and sound-led access | [Audio Descriptions](https://github.com/Double-FW/Audio-Descriptions) | Relevant visual meaning, route decisions and review. Do not assume email clients can play the required media/access track. |
| Captioned linked media | [Closed Caption Subtitles](https://github.com/Double-FW/Closed-Caption-Subtitles) | Speech/sound coverage, timing and player checks; a caption file alone does not establish usable linked playback. |

Use the [workflow/handoff map](Production_Workflow_and_Handoff_Map.md) where a message connects publications and media. Preserve source namespaces rather than flattening guide sections, caption IDs and test IDs into one score.

Use current primary standards and actual delivery evidence for consequential decisions. Double-FW guidance does not amend WCAG or establish legal applicability. If a source becomes unavailable, record the limitation, inspect any relevant public replacement and keep affected conclusions unknown. A retrieval error does not prove a repository is private. Do not reconstruct private material or bulk-copy linked content without an appropriate reuse basis.
