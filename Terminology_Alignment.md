# Terminology alignment

The inspected [Double-FW Terminology and Principles](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles) revision is `219b5feddd271bcdf70ea0ac0c4bc5c62fe9a0f4`. Read its [governance](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles/blob/219b5feddd271bcdf70ea0ac0c4bc5c62fe9a0f4/GOVERNANCE.md) and [decision register](https://github.com/Double-FW/Terminology-and-Principles/blob/219b5feddd271bcdf70ea0ac0c4bc5c62fe9a0f4/review/DECISIONS.md). Approved resolutions are scoped decisions; most individual definitions remain proposed. This alignment does not approve every glossary entry or adopt an organisation's email policy.

## Meanings and experience

| Distinction | Application to email |
| --- | --- |
| Accessibility / inclusion | Address relevant barriers and participation, not only technical availability. A delivered message or installed template does not establish an inclusive experience. |
| Task completion | Verify the intended outcome and relevant consequences. Opening, clicking or submitting does not alone demonstrate correct completion or comprehension. |
| Content consumption / engagement | Investigate access to meaning and the person's chosen involvement. Longer reading time may reflect difficulty rather than value. |
| User preference support | Verify that relevant presentation/interaction choices work in the actual journey. Do not promise a format selector or setting unsupported by the client. |
| Equivalent/comparable experience | State the comparison across outcomes, effort, privacy, availability, control and dignity; identical HTML/plain text formatting is not the goal (D02). |
| Satisfactory / satisfaction | Satisfactory is the proposed Double-FW experience principle; satisfaction is a person's assessment. Ratings do not cancel unmet requirements or barriers (D01). |
| Emotional lenses | Feeling Seen, Feeling Capable, Feeling Confident, Feeling Safe and Feeling Valued are intended qualities and research lenses, not feelings an agent assigns from a technical pass (D03). |

Preserve identity and language preferences, including Deaf and neurodivergent identities. Do not infer diagnosis, literacy, preferences or capability from interaction data or a selected format. Concise copy should not remove essential meaning or flatten identity.

## Authority and identifiers

Use adopted Double-FW meanings for its methodologies and explain a departure from another approach. Preserve source-owned meanings when citing WCAG, law, technical specifications or contracts (D08). A local 14pt baseline, standalone-link preference or line-length aim is not automatically a WCAG requirement. WCAG robustness, Agentic Robustness and reliability of a specific email/client combination have distinct scopes (D06); no general claim demonstrates every client or assistive technology works.

If using cognitive identifiers, name the owning source and use `CP-01`–`CP-10`; bare CP has other meanings (D04). Use ACT-style procedural test for a local structured procedure and user-oriented task script for the relevant complete activity. W3C ACT refers to the actual W3C work and rule status, not any locally generated checklist (D05).

Local numbered guide sections and S001-style retrieval labels are navigation, not external criteria or approval. Keep original source identity and exact revision when comparing interpretations. Record unresolved conflicts rather than silently rewriting an external meaning.

## Evidence and decision states

Keep observations, participant accounts, estimates, hypotheses, agent inferences, illustrative/synthetic examples, proposals and approvals distinct (D07). A filled review template, published repository or export timestamp does not demonstrate an executed check or human approval.

| Evaluation result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Verified | Relevant evidence supports the stated property for the specified artefact, environment and scope. It is not overall conformance or universal access. |
| Failed | Evidence shows the scoped requirement was not met. |
| Unknown | Available evidence has not established the result. |
| Not tested | The specified evaluation did not run. |
| Not applicable | The property does not apply to the stated scope, with a recorded rationale and required decision. |

Unresolved is an open issue/decision, with an underlying result and next action where known. Ready for the next drafting/review step, human acceptance, send authority, dispatch, receipt and recipient outcome are distinct. A successful code check does not establish correct MIME packaging, client presentation, comprehension or a successful downstream task.

Preserve source review, test execution, decision and export dates as different events. A new retrieval or export does not renew an old test or legal verification. Any actual approval needs its authorised decision record, scope and evidence.
